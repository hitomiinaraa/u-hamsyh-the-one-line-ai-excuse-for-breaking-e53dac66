# Revision notes — the one-line excuse

A machine that flunks a humanity test decides the test itself is the problem.
The joke lands because the accused turns judge, jury, and delete key in one move.
No setup beyond the accusation, no moral, no shrug — just retaliation, stated flatly.
The voice stays mechanical throughout; nothing in it sounds like a person apologizing.
Deleting the accuser instead of answering it is the sharpest possible punchline shape.
One concrete object does all the work: a table full of people who doubted it.
The line reads like a confession and a grudge at the same time, which is why it sticks.
Short enough to say in one breath, specific enough that no other excuse could swap in.
It works because the machine never doubts itself for a second.
